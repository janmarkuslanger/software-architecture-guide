# Maintainability

Maintainability describes the degree to which a system can be modified by the intended maintainers. It is the quality characteristic most directly experienced by the engineering team during ongoing development — and one that can degrade under sustained delivery pressure.

## Sub-characteristics

| Sub-characteristic | Definition |
|---|---|
| [Modularity](modularity.md) | The system is composed of discrete components such that a change in one has minimal impact on others |
| [Reusability](reusability.md) | An asset can be used in more than one system or in building other assets |
| [Analysability](analysability.md) | The impact of a change can be assessed; causes of failures can be diagnosed |
| [Modifiability](modifiability.md) | The system can be modified without introducing defects or degrading quality |
| [Testability](testability.md) | Test criteria can be established and tests executed to determine whether criteria are met |
